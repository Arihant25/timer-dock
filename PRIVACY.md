# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Timer Dock does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

## What it stores

The current timer or stopwatch (its length, label, start time and laps) is saved in a small file in the app's own data folder on your PC, so it keeps running after a restart. Uninstalling the app deletes that folder.

## What it does not do

- It makes no network requests. The package doesn't declare internet access.
- It doesn't read any other files or apps.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, ads or third-party services.

It shows a Windows notification when a timer finishes. Notifications are shown by Windows on your PC and aren't sent anywhere.
